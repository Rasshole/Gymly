TALLAGHT GYM

FLYEfit Tallaght is a spacious 30,000 sq ft gym at Tallaght Cross West, just a three-minute walk from The Square Shopping Centre and beside Tallaght Luas stop. The gym features a large blue Astro area, Eleiko equipment, free weights, strength and conditioning facilities, cardio machines, a spin studio and a high-performance area, with free classes available throughout the week.

Members can use two hours of free parking in the Aldi underground car park. Enter your vehicle registration at the pay station when leaving and scan the QR code available in the gym. Please note that the car park closes at 10.30pm.
