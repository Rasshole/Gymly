How To Find our Sandymount, Dublin 4 Health Club

West Wood Club Sandymount is located on St John’s Road, directly opposite the “Martello Tower” on the Sandymount Strand Road.

From Sydney Parade DART station you can cross over the tracks and take the first left on to Park Avenue. Take a right at St. John’s Church and West Wood Club is located on the left hand side at the top of St. John’s Road.

Your membership card will give you access to the car park or press the button for reception. If car park is full, there is plenty of parking available on the Sandymount Strand sea front, just seconds away from our front door.
