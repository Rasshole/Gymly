CRUMLIN GYM

FLYEfit Crumlin is located in the Ashleaf Shopping Centre. This state-of-the-art gym is a Hammer Strength Training Centre featuring everything from a heated chamber to a Yoga/Pilates/Dance studio.

This gym is packed with premium equipment and includes everything from Olympic Lifting platforms to stretching areas and everything in between.

This gym is sure to have something to suit everyone!

Why Choose Our Crumlin Gym?

  • Hammer Strength Training Centre
  • Olympic lifting platforms and premium strength equipment
  • Heated chamber with Heated Polar classes
  • Dedicated yoga, Pilates and dance studio
  • Virtual spinning, boxing and functional training areas
  • Ashleaf Shopping Centre location with free parking
