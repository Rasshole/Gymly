Our Iconic Health Clubs.

Iconic Health Clubs provide a wellness oasis for every member, pairing an unparalleled array of fitness, strength and mobility classes with rejuvenating activities designed to leave them feeling strengthened and refreshed. Distinctly different from the average gym, Iconic Health Clubs deliver professional, person-centered service that results in high participation and satisfied members.

Iconic Health Clubs provide exclusive members-only spaces for Dublin residents to relax, re-energise and refresh. With cutting-edge gym equipment, luxury spa facilities and a dynamic, motivated team, our health clubs are a sanctuary of fitness, wellness and community in the city.

Our Iconic Classes.

Iconic Health Clubs offers a huge selection of the Worlds best fitness classes. All our classes are included in our memberships, so you have the ultimate choice in how you want to train and get fit.
