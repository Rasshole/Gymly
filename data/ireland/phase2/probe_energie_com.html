Zero Attitudes. Zero Intimidation.

No matter where you are on your fitness journey, our gyms are open to everyone. We’re a locally owned community gym built on connection, support, and real people. You’ll find no judgment or negativity here, just a fun, friendly, and relaxed environment.

When you join Energie Fitness, you get access to everything you’d expect from a high-quality training space without the inflated price tag. Our gyms are fully equipped with versatile kits including punch bags, TRX, and more.
