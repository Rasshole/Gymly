Aura Internet designs and develops powerful and interactive websites for a range of small to medium companies and public sector organisations throughout Ireland. We work with our clients to find and develop solutions for their online requirements:

  • allowing them to become more efficient,
  • providing easily accessible solutions for their clients,
  • through increased sales & leads,
  • and by providing very responsive support to their queries.

We specialise in e-commerce websites, offering hosting and a range of online promotion and marketing services to maximise the impact of our customers’ web sites. We pride ourselves on the collaborative relationships we build with clients which allow us to ensure that our website design closely reflect their values and corporate identities.
