FAQ

Where can I find Holmes Place Clubs in Athens?  

Holmes Place Clubs are located in three key areas of Athens, serving both the northern and southern suburbs, as well as the city center. In Maroussi, the Holmes Place gym is situated in the commercial hub of Golden Hall, offering easy access for those living or working in the area. In Glyfada, the club is centrally located on Grigoriou Lambraki, making it ideal for residents of the southern suburbs. Lastly, Holmes Place in Syntagma is in the heart of the city, making it a perfect choice for those living in central Athens. All gyms are fully equipped with modern facilities, offering high-quality fitness, wellness, and spa services.

Which Holmes Place Club is near me?

To find the closest Holmes Place Club to your location, you can visit the Holmes Place website and use the "Choose your Club" feature. This tool allows you to enter your area and find the nearest Holmes Place center. The three clubs in different areas of Athens—Maroussi, Glyfada, and Syntagma—serve various parts of the city. You can choose the gym that is conveniently located near your home or workplace, ensuring easy access and an optimal fitness and wellness experience.

Is there a Holmes Place gym with a swimming pool?

Yes, at Holmes Place Clubs, we offer state-of-the-art facilities that include swimming pools designed to meet the needs of our members. The pool is a central feature of each club, providing opportunities for water-based aerobic exercise, swimming, and other aqua fitness programs that improve fitness without added strain on the joints. Additionally, the pool facilities include dedicated relaxation areas, such as saunas and Jacuzzis, offering a complete wellness experience. We maintain strict cleanliness and upkeep of the pools to ensure the highest standards of hygiene for our members.

Do Holmes Place Clubs offer spa services?

Yes, Holmes Place Clubs provide comprehensive spa services that combine wellness with relaxation. The spa services include a variety of treatments such as massages, facial and body therapies, as well as specialized services like aromatherapy and relaxing massages. The specially designed spa areas offer a serene and calming environment, perfect for unwinding from daily stress. Additionally, the spa services at Holmes Place gyms are tailored to enhance the health and well-being of our members, delivering a complete experience of relaxation and rejuvenation. Our team of expert therapists ensures top-quality services, customized to meet the individual needs of each member.

Can I book a training session with a Personal Trainer at Holmes Place gyms?

Yes, at Holmes Place Clubs, you can book a session with a Personal Trainer who will help you achieve your individual fitness goals. The Personal Trainers at Holmes Place are certified professionals with extensive experience and expertise in various areas of exercise and wellness. Through personalized training programs, the Personal Trainer will safely guide you toward your goals, whether they involve weight loss, muscle strengthening, improving cardiovascular endurance, or overall wellness. Additionally, the Trainers tailor the programs to your individual needs and abilities, offering continuous support and guidance.
