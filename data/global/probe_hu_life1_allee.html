Are you looking for a gym in Budapest district 11?

Life1 Fitness Allee is much more than a conventional gym. This fitness club has one of the widest range of group classes in Budapest 11th district. Professional personal training and spacious, bright interior spaces await you!

Look no further for a gym, visit our fitness club on the 3rd floor of Allee near Kosztolányi Dezső Square, Újbuda Centre and Móricz Zsigmond Boulevard!

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where is the club?

The entrance of our club is located on the 2nd floor of the Allee Shopping Centre, it is easiest to access by the lift on the Bercsényi Street.

2. Is free parking possible?

In the underground car park of Allee Shopping Centre, 2 hours of parking is free of charge for our customers with 8-session and unrestricted season tickets. The parking tickets will be validated at our reception.

3. Is AYCM card accepted?

No and we are not planning to accept it at the Life1 Fitness clubs in the future. SZÉP card payment is possible at our clubs.

4. Is it possible to issue VAT invoice?

Of course! Please indicate if you request invoice to our receptionist prior to purchase!
Unfortunately it is not possible to issue VAT invoice after leaving the cash counter.

5. What outfit is required to use the club?

Using change shoes and towel is mandatory to use the gym.
