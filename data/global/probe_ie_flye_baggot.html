BAGGOT STREET GYM

FLYEfit Baggot Street is located on lower Baggot Street, above Tesco. This gym is in a busy office district and is just 10 minutes away from the Stephens green Luas stop and Pearse street train station.

Go through the door and up the stairs you will be surprised to find a 15,000 sq/ft gym packed full of state of the art equipment for all levels of fitness.

Why Choose Our Baggot Street Gym?

  • 15,000 sq ft gym in Dublin 2
  • Central Baggot Street location
  • Heavy lifting and free weights areas
  • Strength and conditioning Astro area
  • Heated chamber and heated classes
  • Free fitness classes
