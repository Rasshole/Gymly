This is Jims Foetz


Choose more than fitness

At Jims Foetz, we believe in a personalised approach and ongoing support. Our dedicated team is here to motivate you, whatever your level. You'll also find a real community of members who inspire and support each other. Come and experience it for yourself!

Small Group Training

Discover Small Group Training at Jims Foetz: small group sessions (max. 8 people), classified by objective. There's something for everyone! Our coaches will provide you with a targeted programme to help you progress together towards a stronger, healthier and more confident body.

Group classes

Let yourself be carried away by the energy of our group classes at Jims Foetz. From Pilates to BodyPump, including BodyCombat and many others: get moving, progress and have fun in a motivating and friendly atmosphere.

👉 Find out what's on offer below and join us!

A state-of-the-art cardio area

Welcome to our cardio area at Jims: top-of-the-range Life Fitness machines to run, row, walk or cycle at your own pace. With Netflix directly on the screen, your sessions fly by.
☔ Rain or shine? No more excuses: here, you train when you want, in complete freedom and comfort.

The muscle area: your playground for getting stronger.

Here you can lift, push and progress. Enjoy premium Life Fitness and Hammer Strength equipment, whether you're a fan of machines, free weights or bodyweight exercises.

Whatever your level, our coaches are here to guide you and help you push your limits.

Cycling Cube

Discover our interactive cycling courses in the Cycling Cube - the largest cycling studio in Luxembourg.
An immersive experience that stimulates all your senses: lively music, real-time data and an electrifying atmosphere. Let yourself be carried away and go beyond your limits, session after session.
