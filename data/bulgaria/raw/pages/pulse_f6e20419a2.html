COME AND SEE THE CLUB

Pulse MLADOST

Pulse Fitness Mladost combines training , recovery and relaxation in one place. Enjoy a spacious fitness area, group training and one of the most impressive SPA areas in the Mladost area, just minutes from Business Park Sofia.

Working hours: Mon.-Fri. 06:30-22:00 | Sat.-Sun. 09:00-22:00

Area: 5500 sq. m.

Zones: Cardio zone, Strength zone, Functional zone, CrossRX zone, 5D Spinning Studio, Group training rooms, SPA zone

Suitable for: Beginners, advanced, active athletes and teenagers

Parking: Over 250 parking spaces with free parking for up to 3 hours for club visitors.

CONTACTS AND CONNECTION

HALL INFORMATION

  • Ring Road 251A, 1715 Sofia, Bulgaria
  • 0895538787

How to get to Pulse Mladost

  • By car: The club is located on Okolovorasten Pat Blvd., in close proximity to Business Park Sofia and main roads in the Mladost area.
  • From Business Park Sofia: The club can be reached quickly both on foot and by car.
  • By metro: Business Park is the closest metro station to the club.
  • By public transport: Next to the club is the Kino Arena II stop.
  • Parking: The club has over 250 parking spaces and offers free parking for up to 3 hours, which ensures a convenient visit both for training and for using the SPA area.

Contact the club

If you have any questions about membership, group training, personal training or access to the SPA area, contact the Pulse Fitness Mladost team at the indicated phone number - 0895 538 787

Pulse Mladost - One of the largest fitness and SPA complexes in Sofia

If you are looking for a gym in Mladost 4 that combines spacious training areas, modern amenities and recovery opportunities, Pulse Fitness Mladost offers everything you need under one roof. Located near Business Park Sofia, the club impresses with an area of ​​5500 sq. m. , spread over 5 floors.

The large scale of the club means more space for training and less waiting for equipment even during busier hours.

Whether you prefer cardio, strength training, or functional training, here you will find a gym designed to meet different training goals and fitness levels.

One of the distinguishing features of this club is the impressive SPA area . After a workout, you can take advantage of various types of facilities and specially designated spaces for relaxation.

The club has several areas , and the equipment is selected to ensure comfort and efficiency during each workout.

In addition to the diverse training opportunities, visitors can take advantage of additional fitness services such as a massage studio, a sports accessories and nutritional supplements store, as well as spacious recreation areas.

Pulse Fitness Mladost is establishing itself as a preferred choice for people looking for more than a standard fitness club.

INFORMATION

Club areas

Cardio zone

The cardio area offers a variety of equipment for effective workouts and improving endurance.

Functionality: Suitable for burning calories, improving fitness and preparing for more intense workouts.

Equipment and features:

  • Treadmills;
  • Bicycle ergometers;
  • Cross trainers;
  • Spacious training environment;
  • Panoramic views of the area.

Who is it suitable for: For beginners and advanced.

Tip: Use cardio training as a warm-up before working with strength training equipment.

Power zone

The strength zone is located on two floors and offers a variety of fitness equipment for developing strength and muscle mass.

Functionality: Building strength, muscle mass and improving athletic performance.

Equipment and features:

  • Free weights;
  • Shanks and discs;
  • Loungers;
  • Machines for all major muscle groups.
  • Spacious free weight area.

Who is it suitable for: For advanced exercisers and beginners with specific goals.

Tip: Combine exercises with free weights and machines for better results.

Functional area

The functional zone is a space for dynamic workouts that develop strength, mobility, and coordination.

Functionality: Improving general physical fitness and sports skills.

Equipment and features:

  • Free training space;
  • Fitness equipment;
  • Mobility area;
  • Equipment for explosive movements.

Who is it suitable for: For lovers of functional training and an active lifestyle.

Tip: Use the area to finish your workout with a conditioning complex.

Spinning hall

If you love spinning, try group classes in the extremely innovative 5D spinning studio, which will stimulate all your senses during your workout. 

CrossRX Hall

The CrossRX area has over 700 sq. m. of space for functional and strength training.

Functionality: Development of strength, endurance and athletic qualities.

Equipment and features:

  • Olympic bars;
  • Gymnastic exercise area;
  • Powerlifting space;
  • Plyometric equipment;
  • Functional CrossFit corner.

Who is it suitable for: For advanced exercisers and lovers of high-intensity workouts.

Tip: Focus on proper technique before increasing the weights.

Group activity rooms

The two spacious halls offer a variety of group activities for different goals and levels of preparation.

Functionality: More motivation, variety and training under the guidance of an instructor.

Equipment and features:

  • Spacious studios;
  • Various training formats;
  • Professional instructors;
  • Suitable for all levels.

Who they are suitable for: For people who prefer group training.

Tip: Try different classes to find the one that best suits your goals.

Spa area

The SPA area is one of the most impressive spaces in Pulse Mladost and offers conditions for complete relaxation and recovery.

Functionality: Recovery after training, stress reduction and complete rest.

Equipment and features:

  • Jacuzzi;
  • Thermal Roman pool;
  • Shock pool;
  • Panoramic Finnish sauna;
  • Himalayan salt sauna;
  • Relaxation area with fireplace;
  • Massage rooms.

Who is it suitable for: For anyone looking for a relaxation area after work or training.

Tip: Combine a sauna and a shock pool for faster recovery and refreshment during the summer months.

Changing rooms

Pulse Fitness and Spa Mladost offers spacious and modern locker rooms, designed for comfort before and after training.

Functionality: Convenient preparation and refreshment before and after visiting the club.

Equipment and features:

  • Individual lockers;
  • Showers;
  • Spacious rooms;
  • Modern interior.

Who they are suitable for: For all club visitors.

Tip: Each member receives a locker access card at the club. Please remember to collect your personal belongings after each visit to ensure the convenience of all visitors.

5D Spinning Studio

This is an innovative studio that combines cycling with an engaging training atmosphere.

Functionality: Improve endurance and burn a large number of calories.

Equipment and features:

  • Specialized spinning bikes;
  • Modern audio-visual environment;
  • Group formats;
  • Motivating atmosphere.

Who is it suitable for: For all group training enthusiasts.

Tip: Regular spinning workouts are an excellent choice for improving cardio endurance.

Group training at Pulse Mladost

Group training at Pulse Fitness Mladost combines professional leadership , a motivating atmosphere and a variety of training formats.

Whether you want to improve your fitness, burn more calories, or develop strength and mobility, here you will find suitable group activities for the purpose.

How to choose the right workout?

  • Choose Cross Training or Cross Functional Bootcamp for more strength, conditioning, and endurance.
  • Focus on Mobility for better flexibility and recovery.
  • Try Spinning if you're looking for an intense cardio workout.
  • Choose Fit Bands or fit-ball for diverse functional work on the whole body.

Who is this club for?

The club is suitable for anyone who wants to lead an active lifestyle - from beginners to people with specific fitness goals.

For beginners

If you are just starting to train, Pulse Fitness and Spa Mladost is the right place for you to start. The club offers modern equipment and the opportunity for personal and varied training with experienced instructors.

For people with a specific goal

Whether you want to lose weight, increase strength, or improve your mobility, here you will find conditions for effective workouts. Various training zones and professional guidance will help you achieve the desired results.

For fans of group activities

The diverse group training sessions create motivation and a pleasant atmosphere . They are suitable for both beginners and advanced athletes who prefer to train in a team.

For active young people

Pulse is a preferred choice among many young people who lead an active lifestyle. The fitness club in Mladost is suitable for them and offers a modern environment , a variety of activities and opportunities to improve their fitness.

For recovery and relaxation

After a busy workout, you can take time to relax . The club offers a SPA area and a special relaxation area, designed for complete rest and recovery.

The most frequently chosen combinations

Many visitors prefer to combine different workouts and services to achieve better results and a complete fitness experience.

The most commonly chosen combinations are:

  • Strength training and SPA area;
  • CrossRX and recovery in the relaxation area;
  • Group training and cardio zone;
  • Personal training and individual training plan.

They help build good physical shape, maintain motivation and recover faster after exercise. 

Facilities and rules

For a comfortable and pleasant stay, the club offers amenities and clear rules of use.

Club access

Access to the club is via the Pulse Club app . Granting access to another person or entering without registration is not permitted.

Lockers and personal belongings

Each member receives a card with which they can use the lockers available in the club. Personal belongings must be collected after each visit.

Towels

When visiting the club, you must have your own towel . If necessary, a towel can be purchased on site.

Parking

The club has free parking for up to 3 hours . This makes visits easier and saves time searching for a place.

Busy hours

Typically, the busiest hours on weekdays are between 08:00 and 10:00 and between 18:00 and 20:00 . If you prefer a more relaxed training environment, it is advisable to plan your visit outside of these intervals.

Can the club be visited with a CoolFit card?

Pulse Fitness & Spa Mladost works with a CoolFit card. Access and conditions depend on the affiliate program and the type of your card.

Pulse Club Membership

With a club membership , you get access to everything you need to achieve your fitness goals.

It gives you the opportunity to :

  • Access to the club's fitness areas;
  • Various group workouts;
  • Possibility of personal training;
  • Access to the SPA area;
  • Additional club services and extras.

Additional services and the spa area are according to the selected subscription plan.

The tour includes:

  • Brief welcome and introduction;

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit the club without a membership?

A single visit with access to the fitness area, spa area and group training is available for €20.

Can I freeze my membership?

To temporarily freeze your membership, you must submit a request at least 5 days before the due date. This can be done through the Pulse Club app, at reception or by calling 0895 538 787.

Is the club accessible for people with disabilities?

The club has an accessible environment for people with disabilities, and if necessary, assistance from a personal trainer can be provided.

Are there any special events?

During different periods of the year, the club organizes master classes, seminars and other special events.
