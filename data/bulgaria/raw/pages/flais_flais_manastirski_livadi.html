Fitness Flais Manastirski Livadi

Fitness Flais Manastirski Livadi is located at Manastirski Livadi – West, 56 Pirin Str. It is positioned on 2 floors, with 900 sq.m. area.

 

The gym is equipped with professional high-end Precor fitness equipment, more than 40 cardio machines, solarium, fitness stairs and rowing simulators.

 

In our protein & fresh bar you will find a huge variety of high quality nutritional supplements in different categories.

 

The club works with a standard Multisport and Multisport Kids card for group activities.

 

Our highly qualified fitness instructors are always at your disposal, ready to take care of your needs.

 

Come and make your workout a real pleasure!
