Welcome to THE Fitness Jelkovec!

Our club, located on the first floor of Park&Shop Sesvete, spans 1200 m² and is equipped with modern Technogym and Proud equipment! Whether you want to build strength, improve cardiovascular endurance, or enhance your overall health, we have everything you need to achieve your goals. Come and see for yourself!
