24/7 Fitness Club Ta’ Qali

  1. Home
  2. Clubs
  3. 24/7 Fitness Club Ta’ Qali

Spread across three expansive floors with an impressive total area of 1,200 square meters, 24/7 Fitness Club Ta’ Qali stands as the flagship of our chain, operating successfully since 2002.

Committed to a journey of continuous enhancement, the fitness club underwent a comprehensive renovation in 2023. 24/7 Fitness Club Ta’ Qali hosts a vast array of amenities, featuring over 300 state-of-the-art cardiovascular and strength training machines all specifically laid out in specific zones. In addition, our facility offers a fully equipped functional training area for diverse fitness classes. State-of-the-art locker rooms, thoughtfully designed with your comfort in mind, along with a luxurious sauna room to unwind post workout. Nestled in close proximity to Ta’ Qali’s national park, our fitness club offers an ideal setting for an invigorating post-workout run amidst nature. With ample parking space, we ensure that every visit is a stress-free and convenient experience for our members.
