Marsa Sports Complex

Home Facilities Marsa Sports Complex

The Marsa Sport Complex is split into two blocks.  These include Multi-Purpose halls, squash courts, a weightlifting centre run by the national association, two outdoor netball courts and a fitness centre (privately managed).   The block also includes offices for the sports associations using the premises. 

The Facility is undergoing construction. For services available, please contact the manager.
