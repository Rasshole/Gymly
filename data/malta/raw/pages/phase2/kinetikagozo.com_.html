Why Choose Kinetika?

IMPRESSIVE FACILITY & FUN ENVIRONMENT

As the first gym chain on the island, our two facilities complete each other perfectly, offering the best gym experience for every need. Our gym staff is qualified and always happy to help, contributing to supportive and enjoyable training environment.

CERTIFIED PROFESSIONAL PERSONAL TRAINERS

Our inpiring personal trainers have very wide-ranging areas of expertise, which makes finding your personal fit easy! From pre/post-natal training to endurance training, from HIIT to strength training, our qualified team covers all the bases.

CUSTOMISED TRAINING, MAXIMUM RESULTS

Our facilities provide an excellent choice of different training equipment. Deadlift platforms, heavy duty squat racks, selection of different bench setups - incline, decline, flat - for heavy lifters, different cardio machines for endurance and HIIT training, functional area with boxing equipment and even football pitch to name a few.. perfect conditions for every type of training!
