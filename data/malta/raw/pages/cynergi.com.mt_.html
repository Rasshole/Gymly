Why Cynergi

Client Focused Team

Our staff and trainers put you first. Whether it’s personal training, physiotherapy, or guidance, our fitness experts help you succeed every step of the way.

Be Part of a Community

Our community has grown these past 30 years into a fitness family. Connect, train, and grow together at the most vibrant health & fitness club in Malta. Welcome to the club.

State-of-the-Art

Train with world-class equipment in Malta’s top gym. Spacious areas, advanced machines, and expert support are everything you need to smash your fitness goals.

All-inclusive membership

One membership, everything included: gym, classes, pool, sauna, ice bath and more. The best value fitness club in Malta, designed to keep you motivated every day.

Flexible Options

Choose the gym membership that fits your lifestyle. Flexible plans, no hidden fees. Find the perfect membership & enjoy fitness on your terms.
